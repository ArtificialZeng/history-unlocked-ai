This module modifies the licensed data by selecting integer-ID FORM rows, NFKD/lowercase ASCII-letter normalization, removing word boundaries, extracting six bounded development excerpts, seeded substitution and shuffle transformations, and computing training-letter frequencies and a smoothed quadgram model. These corpus-derived artifacts and project contributions to them are distributed under the same **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** conditions: `frozen/inference_inputs/`, `frozen/grading_gold/` and `frozen/inference_outputs/`. Generated corpus/model/control/replay data also retain those conditions. Attribution and licence notices must travel with redistributed data. No upstream author or institution endorses this project.

The full raw corpus is not bundled. `sources.json` records immutable source URLs, the original access timestamps, bytes and hashes.
