The data transformations select integer-ID `FORM` rows, normalize NFKD/lowercase ASCII letters, remove boundaries, extract six bounded development excerpts, apply seeded substitutions, collapse seeded unknown runs and singleton erasures to sentinel 26, shuffle observed symbols while preserving gaps, and compute training-letter frequencies and a smoothed quadgram model. Every mask retains original coordinates and actual length in separate gold. Corpus-derived artifacts and project contributions to them are distributed under the same **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** terms, including all of `frozen/` and the corpus-derived audit aggregate data. New raw corpus, rebuilt model, fixture, baseline, replay and grading data also retain those terms. Attribution and licence notices must travel with redistributed data. No upstream author or institution endorses this project.

The two large raw corpus files are not bundled. `sources.json` preserves immutable source URLs, original access timestamps, bytes and hashes. The model is byte identical to the existing public Czech wordless module: SHA-256 `64f3e69c72e7e74f5c7fd3e41c90c42321ac46879d91f3b52d6f0182c4db8a64`, 2,125,764 bytes in original little-endian float32 layout.
