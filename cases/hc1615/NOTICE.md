The full picture/address scans and source crop images are excluded from the default distribution. Original URLs, acquisition receipts, byte/RGB hashes and an explicit fetch/replay helper are included. Consult the original provider for image reuse terms.

DejaVu 2.37 fonts are unmodified official-release files. Their original license and provenance are in`assets/fonts/`. The note’s small diagram is an author-created schematic of the tested hypothesis, not a tracing of the historical handwriting.

The old-case dictionary is included solely as the authorized prior alphabet with its original byte pin. Other puzzles’ full ciphertext, plaintext and solver models are excluded. Source alternatives and the baseline failure remain part of the evidence. This package has not been externally adjudicated or uploaded by the local build.
